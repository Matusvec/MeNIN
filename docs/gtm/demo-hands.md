# Demo hands: what Moss deals for the three demo themes

Sampled from the production dealer (`HOMER_DEALER=moss` on the droplet, the 213-formula bank) on Oct 24 at 10:30 AM PT, for the three theme cards in [demo-script.md](demo-script.md). Rehearse on these and flag any hand that reads badly to the leader.

How a battle round is dealt, so you know what changes from one run to the next:

- Moss ranks the whole bank by meaning against the theme. Each hand is drawn at random from the closest matches, so **no two rounds deal the same hand**. Rehearse on the kind of cards a theme deals, not on one exact hand.
- Every hand holds at least one half-line (marked ½, it fills half a line: two hits), one epithet and one kenning.
- The two poets in a round never share a card. In a Rematch, a poet never gets a card they already held.
- If Moss is ever slow or down, the old tag dealer deals instead, with no error and no pause. One sample of that is under each theme.
