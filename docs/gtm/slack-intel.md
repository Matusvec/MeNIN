# Slack intel

A running log from the Cal Hacks Slack, kept by gtm until Sun 9 AM. **Newest first.** Sweeps ran about every 30 minutes until Sat 10:15 AM, then every 2 hours. Anything that changes a deadline, rule or judging format, or any high-value opportunity, goes to leader immediately; the rest is batched here.

**Sources are cited by channel, time and role only (organizer, sponsor staff, a hacker), never by a person's name**, because this repo goes public on Sunday.

Read-only: we never post, reply or react in Slack. Anything we'd need to say there gets drafted and sent to leader for Matus to approve and post. Other teams' names and details stay out of this file.

## What wins each track: the hints in Slack

Read directly from the channels, Sat 9 AM. Each hint is quoted, and each one says what we do about it.

**Spacetime.** Their CEO: "Something that's hard to build without Spacetime!" (#0-general, Fri 6:52 PM). The guide: Spacetime as the core real-time backend, "not just added on the side".
- **Do:** show a moment that's hard without it. Kill the app on one phone mid-battle and reopen it: the poet takes their seat back with their score, because a new identity with the same name takes over a disconnected seat (spacetime/CONTRACT.md, `join_room`). It's tested in multiplayer's check runs or take 2 of the evening shoot (on Matus's "done" call), and shown only if it passes.
- **Do:** say "private hands in a private table, the whole battle as a server-side phase machine, a scheduled reducer for the turn timer".
- **Do:** go to their workshop, 4–5 PM in the Theater, since Spacetime staff judge the track.

**Anthropic, new (Sat 10:07 AM, #cohost-anthropic):** their 1 PM workshop is on multi-agent teams in Claude Code, which is how MeNIN was built. Bring one concrete line about our setup (one spec, one folder per session, a leader that commits) to the Anthropic pitch, and go to the workshop.

**Anthropic, new (Sat 7:49 PM, #cohost-anthropic, the track's judge):** "There's no *main* criteria. All four we listed are equally important." - "The narrower, the better."
- "'Students' is too broad."
- "we'll prefer if you built things more creative": their example ideas are utilities, so a creative project is welcome.

- **Do:** name both kinds of use: Claude in the product (judge, coach, MC, song) and in development (a team of Claude Code sessions).
